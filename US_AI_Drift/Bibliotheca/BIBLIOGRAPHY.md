# Bibliotheca — US_AI_Drift

**A second, self-contained hypothesis.** This folder mirrors the repository's own `Bibliotheca/`,
`Universe/` and `OBJECTIVE.md`, scoped to a different idea, a different market and a different
universe. It does not read from or write to the root `Bibliotheca/`, and the root files (the
Volkswagen-relative hypothesis) are unaffected by anything in here. See `../README.md` for why two
hypotheses live in one repository.

The format follows the root `Bibliotheca/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`: a note per source, with a heading that
states the source's own claim and a blockquote for what it implies for this strategy.
