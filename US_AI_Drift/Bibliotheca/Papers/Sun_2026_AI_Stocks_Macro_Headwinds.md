# After rising 3,070% (Nvidia) and 207% (Microsoft) from 2020 through 2025, both stocks stalled in 2026 — Nvidia up only 8%, Microsoft down nearly 60% — on inflation, rate and geopolitical concerns unrelated to either company's earnings

The article reports that from the start of 2020 to the end of 2025, Nvidia's stock rose 3,070% and
Microsoft's 207%, a run coinciding with the AI theme this universe is built around. In 2026 that
reversed sharply: Nvidia up only 8% and Microsoft down nearly 60% for the year, which the author
attributes to inflation concerns, the Federal Reserve's reluctance to cut rates, and an escalating
Middle East conflict — macroeconomic and geopolitical pressures that cooled the broader AI rally,
not a change in either company's underlying AI business.

> **This is the argument against the thesis, kept in rather than dropped.** If a macro regime can
> overwhelm two of the most AI-exposed names in the universe this hard, in a year with no shortage
> of real earnings beats (Nvidia's November 2025 report, logged separately in this Bibliotheca,
> falls inside this same stretch), then any measured drift has to survive being run through
> attribution before it means anything — a positive raw spread in 2026 could easily be "this
> universe has high market beta and beta was punished," not "beats keep under-reacting." This is
> exactly why `OBJECTIVE.md`'s kill switch requires the spread to survive attribution and not just
> be positive on its own, and it is the reason a single macro-heavy year like 2026 should not be
> read as a clean test of the underreaction claim by itself.
