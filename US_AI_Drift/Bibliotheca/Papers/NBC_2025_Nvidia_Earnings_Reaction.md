# Nvidia's shares rose more than 4% in after-hours trading following an earnings report analysts called "well above Street whisper numbers"

Reporting live on Nvidia's earnings call, NBC News quotes CEO Jensen Huang saying demand for
Blackwell AI chips is "off the charts" and that another processor line is "sold out," and quotes
analyst Dan Ives calling it "a golden quarter for Nvidia with demand massive and well above Street
whisper numbers." The immediate market reaction was a rise of more than 4% in after-hours trading,
with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq both indicated higher ahead of the next day's open.

> This is the day-zero reaction the test's event window starts from, not the drift itself. PEAD

> that more of the same-direction move should show up over the following one to three months. This
> article establishes that a real, large, AI-specific beat happened on a known date with a known
