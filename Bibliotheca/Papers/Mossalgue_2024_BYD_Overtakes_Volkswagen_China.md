# BYD ended Volkswagen's 15-year run as China's top-selling car brand in 2023, the first time any brand had outsold VW there since 2008

Citing two independent counts — CATARC new-registration data (BYD 2.4 million / 11% share versus
VW 2.3 million / 10.27%) and CPCA's own count (BYD 2.57 million / 11.85% versus VW 2.3 million, down
0.2% year on year) — the article reports that BYD passed Volkswagen as China's best-selling car
brand for full-year 2023. It attributes the shift to Chinese buyers preferring "cheap, high-tech
homegrown EVs," and notes VW's response (an entry-level EV platform, a partnership with Xpeng) as
still in progress rather than having turned the trend around at the time of writing.

> This is the direct evidence for the "China" half of the edge claimed in `OBJECTIVE.md`: not a
> single bad quarter, but the end of a fifteen-year position, dated to a specific year with two
> independently sourced counts that agree with each other. What it does **not** show is the thing
> the objective actually tests — whether this reached VW's *share price* later than it reached these
> registration counts. That gap, not the decline itself, is the mispricing being claimed, and this
> source only establishes the first half of it.
