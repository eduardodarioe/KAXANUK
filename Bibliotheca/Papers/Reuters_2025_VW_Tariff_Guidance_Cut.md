# Volkswagen cut its 2025 operating-margin guidance from 5.5-6.5% to 4-5%, after a €1.3 billion first-half hit from US tariffs

Reuters reports that Volkswagen lowered its full-year 2025 guidance twice in one release: the
operating-margin range moved from 5.5-6.5% down to 4-5%, and the sales-growth guidance moved from
"up to 5% higher than the prior year" down to "level with the prior year." The company attributes
the cut to three things together, not one: US tariffs (a €1.3 billion / $1.5 billion hit in the
first half alone), restructuring expenses, and higher sales of lower-margin electric vehicles.
Finance chief Arno Antlitz is quoted saying a Japan-style 15% tariff deal would put margins near the
middle of the new range, and that "the clock was ticking" on a trade agreement before August 1.
