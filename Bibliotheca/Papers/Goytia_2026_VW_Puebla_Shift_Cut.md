# Volkswagen eliminated the night shift at its Puebla plant, affecting an estimated 786 to 1,350 workers, as US tariffs cut export demand

Volkswagen indefinitely removed the third, night-time shift building the Tiguan and Jetta at its
Puebla plant. The worker count is not officially confirmed: VW itself gave no figure, one report
cites 786 directly affected, and local media put it between 1,300 and 1,350. The article ties the
cut to a 25% US tariff on vehicles and parts, active since April 2025, and to Inegi data showing
Mexican light-vehicle exports down 9.69% year on year in July 2026. The union, SITIAVW, issued a
rejection statement the same day the cut was reported.
